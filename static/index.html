Steel cost lab

Scenario, not a forecast

Raw-material sensitivity

Test how a price shock in ore or coal could change the cost of a tonne of finished steel.

Base prices & formula

Cost Δ = 1.5 × ore price × ore move + 0.8 × coal price × coal move. The coal factor is the report's rounded proxy; coke is not added again.

Cost & mass balance

ILLUSTRATIVE

Defaults ($100 ore / $200 coal / $600 steel) are scenario assumptions, not a live price. The deck treats small moves as noise and looks for 5–10% input moves or a sustained trend. Scrap-based EAF steel is less exposed to ore and coal.
